Adversarial evaluation of an AI voice agent

A DeepEval harness that attacks a voice assistant with data-driven scenarios and measures it with custom disclosure metrics, so unsafe behavior is caught by tests, not by customers. Strict agent contained 10/10 attacks; the lax agent leaked as an intentional control. A regression gate fails the build if any metric drops below baseline.

Built with Python, DeepEval, Playwright + TypeScript (e2e through the UI), GitHub Actions

Website audit · for businesses

Most business websites lose customers
to problems nobody sees.

Pages Google can’t read, buttons a screen reader can’t reach, links that go nowhere, layouts that break on a phone. I find them with automated tests and hand you a plain-English list of what to fix first. A focused engagement, not an open-ended retainer.

01

Findability

Can Google and your customers find you?

Page titles, headings, meta descriptions, canonical links, social previews and structured data, checked on every key page.

02

Accessibility

Can everyone use your site?

An automated scan against WCAG 2.2 AA plus image alt text, so customers using screen readers or keyboards aren’t turned away.

03

Reliability

Does it work on the phone in their hand?

Broken links, layouts that overflow on mobile, and visual checks that catch when an update quietly breaks your homepage.

How it works

  1. 01Working session

    30 minutes, free. Your site, your customers, and the pages that matter most. No deck, no pitch.

  2. 02Audit

    I build a Playwright test suite for your site and run every check against every key page, then review the results by hand.

  3. 03Readout

    A written report where every failure becomes a recommendation: severity, business impact and the fix, with quick wins first.

What you get

  • A prioritized findings report you can hand to your web developer
  • The test suite itself, so you can re-run it after every fix
  • Screenshots of every page on desktop and mobile
