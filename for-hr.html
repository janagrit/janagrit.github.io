Senior SDET at Cooley

Playwright · TypeScript · role-based E2E testing

I’m a Senior SDET at Cooley, working on Vanilla, a fund-transfer platform.

The situation

I inherited a QA project that was behind and had been through two engineer departures. I reverse-engineered the undocumented business logic and rebuilt the test framework in Playwright and TypeScript.

The hard part

Role-based testing. Transfers pass through five stages, and what each persona can do changes at every stage.

What I built

An end-to-end framework that covers six transfer scenarios across 56 test cases. I also documented the framework so the team can onboard without relying on me.

  • 6transfer scenarios
  • 56test cases
  • ~20 minfull run
  • 6bugs caught before release
